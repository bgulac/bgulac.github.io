Namaz Vaktim: Prayer Times & Adhan

Namaz Vaktim (Turkish for "prayer time") is a prayer times and worship app for Muslims in Turkey and Europe. Diyanet prayer times, adhan, Qibla, Quran, makruh times, missed prayer and fasting tracking, and the AI assistant AI Hoca that answers with sources, all in one app. For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Prayer times and adhan: Daily, weekly and monthly views, adhan and reminders for every prayer, home screen widgets.
  • Diyanet times: Diyanet tables for Turkey and Europe. Your location is detected automatically, or you can pick a city manually.
  • Qibla: By map, compass or camera.
  • Quran: Mushaf with tajweed colors, audio recitation, translation and tafsir summaries.
  • Makruh times: Optionally shown as a red slice in the clock view.
  • Missed prayers and fasting: Track your qada debt prayer by prayer and record missed fasts by year.
  • AI Hoca: Answers from sources such as Diyanet fatwas, hadith, duas and Quran translations. With your confirmation it sets up adhan, reminders and qada for you.
  • Question of the day, achievements and a leaderboard.

For Muslims living in Europe

If your location is in Europe, the Diyanet Europe times are selected automatically, and you can choose cities in Germany separately. The app is available in Turkish, German and English. German page: Namaz Vaktim: Gebetszeiten. Turkish page: Namaz Vaktim: Namaz Vakitleri.

Frequently asked questions

Which calculation method does Namaz Vaktim use?

The default is the Diyanet table for Turkey and the Diyanet Europe table for Europe. In Settings you can also choose other methods such as Umm al-Qura (Makkah), Egyptian, Karachi and Tehran.

Can I use it in Germany and the rest of Europe?

Yes. If your location is in Europe, the Diyanet Europe times are selected automatically. For Germany you can also choose your city. The app interface is in Turkish, German and English.

How are makruh times shown in the app?

You can turn them on in Settings. They appear as a red slice in the clock view.

How do I track my missed prayers?

On the Qada page you record your debt for each prayer. Each time you pray one, the count goes down by one.

What is AI Hoca?

AI Hoca is an AI assistant that answers with sources. It can make mistakes, so please consult a qualified scholar for important religious decisions.

Is the app free?

The core features are free and include ads. An ad-free experience and extra sounds are available as an optional subscription.
