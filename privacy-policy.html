Introduction

Bayram Gulac ("we", "us", "our") built the Namaz Vaktim: Ezan, Kuran application (the "App") as a free, ad-supported application.   • AlQuran.Cloud API: For fetching Quran content. No personal data is sent to this service.

AI Hoca (AI-assisted Q&A)

When you type a question into AI Hoca, your question and the last few messages of the conversation are sent to our servers and to our infrastructure provider Cloudflare (USA) in order to prepare an answer. We do not store your question texts linked to your account. We only keep a daily counter tied to your anonymous account ID so that we can apply the daily question limit.

If you mark an answer as "Not helpful", that question, the answer and the list of sources used are sent to us to review and improve the answer, and are stored together with your account ID. Choosing "Helpful" sends no data. You may request deletion of these records by contacting us (see Contact Us).

Answers are generated by AI from Diyanet fatwas and publications, Qur'an translation and commentary, and hadith and prayer sources. They may contain errors and are not binding religious rulings; for important matters, please also ask a religious scholar you trust. When you are offline or the feature is turned off, the App uses the built-in answers on your device and sends no data.

Live Broadcasts

The App displays live video/audio broadcasts from the Two Holy Mosques (Mecca and Medina). The App does not host, record, or redistribute these broadcasts. Streams are loaded in real-time directly from publicly accessible endpoints operated by official Islamic authorities, including the Saudi General Presidency for the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques (live.net.sa). When the primary stream is unavailable, the App may fall back to embedding a public YouTube live stream of the same source. The App is not responsible for the content, continuity, or quality of these broadcasts. Stream providers may discontinue access at their discretion. No personal data is transmitted to broadcast endpoints beyond what is technically required for HTTP/HLS streaming (IP address, basic device user-agent).

Data Storage and Security

Features that need an account or a connection are stored on Google Firebase servers (Firestore): your account (anonymous, or email, Google or Apple sign-in), username and email address if you create one, notification token and language, leaderboard points, community (hatim and dhikr group) participation, daily question answers, support messages and feedback, and subscription status. AI Hoca stores only a daily question counter linked to your account, plus any answer you mark as "Not helpful" (see the AI Hoca section). You can request deletion of this data by contacting us (see Contact Us).

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

AI Hoca (yapay zekâ destekli soru-cevap)

AI Hoca'ya bir soru yazdığınızda, sorunuz ve sohbetteki son birkaç mesajınız cevap hazırlanması amacıyla sunucularımıza ve altyapı sağlayıcımız Cloudflare'e (ABD) iletilir. Soru metinlerinizi hesabınızla ilişkilendirerek saklamıyoruz. Yalnızca günlük soru hakkınızı uygulayabilmek için, anonim hesap kimliğinize bağlı bir günlük sayaç tutuyoruz.

Bir cevabı "Beğenmedim" ile işaretlerseniz, o soru, cevap ve cevapta kullanılan kaynakların listesi, cevabı incelemek ve iyileştirmek amacıyla bize gönderilir ve hesap kimliğinizle birlikte saklanır. "Beğendim" seçeneği hiçbir veri göndermez. Bu kayıtların silinmesini İletişim bölümündeki adresten talep edebilirsiniz.

Cevaplar yapay zekâ tarafından, Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı fetvaları ve yayınları, Kur'an meali ve tefsiri, hadis ve dua kaynaklarından hazırlanır. Hata içerebilir ve bağlayıcı bir dinî hüküm değildir; önemli konularda güvendiğiniz bir din âliminin görüşünü de alınız. İnternet bağlantısı yoksa ya da özellik kapalıysa uygulama cihazınızdaki hazır cevapları kullanır ve hiçbir veri göndermez.
